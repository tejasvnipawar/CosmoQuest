Exo Planet and moons and Galaxies and stars

Exoplanets orbit distant stars, some resembling Earth, while others are scorching hot gas giants or icy super-Earths. Scientists use telescopes like Kepler and James Webb to study their atmospheres, searching for water, oxygen, and signs of alien life. The diversity of these planets challenges our understanding of the cosmos."
"Galaxies are vast cosmic structures containing billions of stars, planets, and dark matter. The Milky Way, a spiral galaxy, spans over 100,000 light-years across."
Galaxies are immense cosmic cities made of billions of stars, planets, and nebulae. The Milky Way is a spiral galaxy, but others include elliptical and irregular types. Some galaxies collide, forming new star systems. Supermassive black holes reside at their centers, shaping their evolution over billions of years."
"Stars begin as nebulae, collapsing under gravity to ignite nuclear fusion. Some, like red dwarfs, burn for trillions of years, while massive stars explode as supernovae, scattering elements across space. The Sun, our life-giving star, is a main-sequence star that provides warmth, energy, and light to our planet."
